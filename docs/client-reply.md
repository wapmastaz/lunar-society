## 1. Direction for the site

I'm building the site around the version in your newest FAQ: **Mr SuperTrump's Lunar Society Founders Plaque**. It's a fun, crowd-powered project from the podcast. The Moon is the goal, and the studio wall is the honorable backup. That version is honest and still exciting. To keep the whole project consistent, I've made these changes to the copy:

- **The Moon is a goal, never a guarantee.** No promises of a "2028–2030 Lunar Base," no names "on the lunar surface permanently," and no "exact coordinates" map. Wherever the Moon comes up, the copy says we're aiming for a commercial payload slot, with the studio wall as the backup.
- **Real scarcity only.** The site shows a live counter of slots actually claimed. "Filling rapidly" appears only when it's true, and tiers lock automatically when they sell out.
- **Pebble wording.** If the pebble is still part of the package, we describe it exactly as it is (for example "commemorative lunar-simulant stone"). We don't say "not from Earth" or "probably from an asteroid" unless your supplier can document it.

- **About page.** I'd leave out "THE GREAT CONN." Next to a sales page, it reads the wrong way.
- **Listener numbers.** "Thousands of listeners" should match your real numbers.
- **Disclaimer.** I'm using your disclaimer exactly as written. It sits in the footer of every page as plain text, plus the unticked checkbox at checkout.

I'd also suggest a quick look from your lawyer at two things: the "Trumpian" naming, and the 5% set-aside wording (promising part of the sales to a cause can come with registration rules).
