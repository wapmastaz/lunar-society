## FAQ (merged)

**What is the Founders Plaque?**
A custom 11.5" x 8.5" plaque made of aerospace-grade 6061-T6 aluminum, laser engraved with the names of exactly 1,000 supporters and listeners of Adventures With Mr SuperTrump.

**Is my name really going to the Moon?**
That's our goal, not a guarantee. We intend to pursue a commercial lunar payload slot as commercial Moon missions mature, likely in the mid-2030s. Space timelines and payload costs are outside our control. If the plaque can't fly, it will be permanently displayed in the Adventures With Mr SuperTrump studio. Either way, your name is on it.

**When will the plaque be made?**
Once all 1,000 slots are filled. We're targeting 2027 to 2028 and will unveil it live on the podcast.

**How do I make sure my name is spelled right?**
Check your confirmation email. Reply before the lock date (48 hours after confirmation) to fix a typo. After that, names are locked for engraving.

**Can I put two names or a family name in one slot?**
Yes, for a +$30 add-on. Accepted formats include Mr & Mrs John Doe, Mr & Mrs J Doe, Mr & Mrs Doe, John & Jane Doe, Doe Family and John Doe Family.

**What's the difference between Alpha and Standard?**
Alpha Founders (#001 to #050) are placed in the top rows of the plaque and receive a gold-foil Alpha certificate. Standard Founders are #051 to #1000.

**Is this affiliated with President Trump or NASA?**
No. See the disclaimer at the bottom of every page.

**[CONFIRM] What is the space pebble?**
A commemorative lunar-simulant stone, beautifully packaged for gifting. It is not lunar material.

**[CONFIRM] Can I send it as a Christmas gift?**
Yes. Enter the recipient's address in PayPal at checkout. There's no price receipt in the box. Order by December 12 for standard shipping or December 18 for expedited shipping. If you miss the cutoff, you'll still receive a printable digital certificate to give on Christmas morning.

**What if timelines change?**
Space projects depend on things outside our control, so dates may shift. We'll share every update on the podcast and by email.
