## What we changed

| Original | On the site | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "prestigious British 19th century tradition" | "the Lunar Society of Birmingham, the celebrated 18th-century circle of British inventors and thinkers" | The Lunar Society of Birmingham met from about 1765 to 1813. That's the 18th century. |
| "a contribution of a few hundred dollars" | "$149, or $199 for an Alpha slot" | It's a purchase at fixed prices. Calling it a "contribution" can read as a donation, and that causes refund disputes. |
| "The plaque is destined for the lunar South Pole, placed directly within the boundaries of the future … Moon Base" | "Our goal is the resource-rich lunar South Pole … No such base exists yet, and no flight can be guaranteed." | Stating a delivery as fact is what PayPal and the FTC treat as misleading. Saying "goal" keeps the excitement and stays honest. |
| "a definitive timeline: securing installation by January 18, 2029" | "MJ Schultz has set January 18, 2029 as the society's target … permanent lunar bases are planned for the 2030s" | "Definitive" is a promise nobody can keep. It's shown as MJ's target, with the studio fallback right next to it. |
| "How will the plaque get to the moon? It is designed to be transported … and installed …" | Same answer, framed as the goal, with the fallback included | Same reason as above. |
| "accurately predicting political shifts" | "to comment on (and, he would say, predict) political shifts" | It keeps the claim as MJ's own playful boast rather than a stated fact. |
| "Beehive Newsletters" | "the Lunar Society newsletter on Beehiiv" | The platform is spelled Beehiiv. |
| Long dashes | Commas and periods | House style for the site. |
